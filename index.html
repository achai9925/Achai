Research & Genomics

Exploring the Role of Tandem Repeat Expansions in the Genetic Etiology of Neural Tube Defects

Medical school capstone · Presented at the American Society of Human Genetics (ASHG) Annual Meeting · Reviewers’ Choice Award

Neural tube defects (NTDs) are complex congenital conditions whose genetic contributors may extend beyond canonical single-nucleotide variants (SNVs). This project investigates non-coding and structural variation by profiling short tandem repeat (STR) expansions in short-read whole-genome sequencing data from two case-control cohorts.

The poster reports cohorts of 49 cases and 53 controls, and 67 cases and 96 controls. It highlights repeat expansions in IFT88 in approximately 9% of cases, alongside other prioritized candidate genes. These are discovery findings for further study, not established causal variants.

STR genomic pipeline

  1. Targeted profiling — ExpansionHunter. Profiled known pathogenic and polymorphic STR loci, estimating repeat size, motif interruptions, zygosity, and statistical confidence bounds.
  2. Genome-wide discovery — ExpansionHunter Denovo (EHdn). Searched for candidate expansions without requiring predefined loci, using paired-end alignment evidence such as anchored reads and spanning read pairs to detect motif outliers and novel expanded alleles.
  3. Visual validation — REviewer. Reviewed candidate loci in BAM/CRAM alignments, inspecting read evidence, phasing, and base quality in repetitive regions.
  4. Filter and prioritize — RExPRT, R, and HPC. Applied genomic quality control and cross-cohort analyses, then used SVM and XGBoost pathogenicity predictions to prioritize candidates for follow-up.

Automation & infrastructure

Built Bash and Python pipelines for high-throughput batch execution on high-performance computing clusters, including data normalization, filtering, and cross-cohort statistical association testing.

Interpretation & next steps

Candidate loci were considered in the context of neural development and biological pathways. Future directions include inheritance analysis in parent-child trios, functional validation, and long-read sequencing to better resolve repeat structure.
