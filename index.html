02 / Research & genomics

Looking beyond the usual variants.

My medical school capstone explores tandem repeat expansions as an underrecognized source of genetic variation in neural tube defects, pairing short-read whole-genome sequencing with locus-level discovery and computational prioritization.

Featured capstone · Computational genomics

Exploring the Role of Tandem Repeat Expansions in the Genetic Etiology of Neural Tube Defects

Presented at the American Society of Human Genetics (ASHG) Annual Meeting

ASHG
Reviewers’ Choice Award

Neural tube defects are complex congenital conditions with genetic contributors that extend beyond canonical single-nucleotide variants. This project investigates non-coding and structural variation by profiling known repeat loci and searching genome-wide for candidate expansions across case-control cohorts.

Short-read WGSPaired-end sequencing data across case-control cohorts
Two cohorts49 and 67 cases; 53 and 96 controls
Beyond SNVsKnown and novel tandem repeat expansion candidates

STR genomic pipeline

PROFILE → DISCOVER → REVIEW → PRIORITIZE

  1. Targeted profiling
    ExpansionHunter

    Profiles known pathogenic and polymorphic loci; estimates repeat size, motif interruptions, zygosity, and confidence bounds.

  2. Genome-wide discovery
    ExpansionHunter Denovo

    Finds candidate expansions without predefined loci by examining anchored reads and spanning read pairs for motif outliers.

  3. Read-level review
    REviewer

    Visually validates candidate loci in BAM/CRAM alignments, including read evidence, phasing, and base quality in repetitive regions.

  4. Filter & prioritize
    RExPRT · R · HPC

    Combines genomic quality control, cross-cohort analyses, and SVM/XGBoost pathogenicity prediction to prioritize candidates.

Automation & infrastructure

Built custom Bash and Python pipelines for high-throughput batch execution on high-performance compute clusters, including data normalization, filtering, and cross-cohort statistical association testing.

Interpretation

Candidate loci were evaluated for expansion evidence and predicted pathogenicity, then considered in the context of neural development and biological pathways.

Why it matters

The work supports tandem repeats as a potentially underrecognized class of variation in NTD genetics and motivates deeper study of prioritized loci.

Next questions

Future directions include inheritance analysis in parent-child trios, functional validation, and long-read sequencing to resolve repeat structure.

Poster highlights · candidates for follow-up

The poster illustrates IFT88 repeat expansions in approximately 9% of cases and prioritizes candidate genes across cohorts. These are discovery findings for further investigation, not established causal variants.
