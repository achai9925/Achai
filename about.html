About

I’m Alan Chai, a medical student at the Frank H. Netter MD School of Medicine at Quinnipiac University, pursuing a future in anesthesiology.

Before medical school, I studied biochemistry and molecular biology with a minor in computer science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, graduating magna cum laude. I also worked in chemical engineering at MilliporeSigma and genomic process development at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard.

Those experiences shaped my interest in using engineering and computational approaches to ask clinical questions. My current research interests include tandem repeat variation in neural tube defects, perioperative physiology, point-of-care ultrasound, and how evidence from around the world can inspire new anesthesia research.

Outside of medicine, I enjoy Chinese yo-yo (diabolo) choreography and instruction, rock climbing, skiing, and piano.
